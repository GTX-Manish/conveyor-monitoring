# Evaluation report

Generated by `python -m scripts.score`. Do not edit by hand.

- Scores are **per event and per alert**, not per row. An event is detected when an alert opens on its asset between the fault's onset and its failure. An alert is false when it opens while the asset's real stage is `normal`.
- Thresholds are tuned on the validation scenarios for a precision of at least 90% (at most 1 to 2 false alerts in 20). Among thresholds that meet it and detect the most events, the most cautious (highest) one that keeps at least 75% of the best lead time is taken, then frozen. Test and stress are scored once.
- Precision here is detected events / (detected events + false alerts). Several alerts on one fault count once, so it is stricter than the false share of all alerts.
- The same alert rules apply to every method: open after 2 windows above the threshold, close after 6 below, hold while the belt is stopped.

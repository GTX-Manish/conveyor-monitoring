## New features

The model now looks at drift in two ways it could not before:

- **Spread-normalised deviation:** each zone's deviation is divided by the spread of all zones in the same window. A fault stands out against the others, while drift lifts everything.
- **Signal agreement:** how many of heat, noise, impact spikes and vibration move together. A drifting sensor moves one signal on its own; a fault moves several.

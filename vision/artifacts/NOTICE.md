# Crack detector model: notice

`best.onnx` is a YOLOv8 crack detector trained on BeltCrack by **Julián Neira Nova**, taken unchanged from
[github.com/JSNN15/conveyor_crack_detection_2026](https://github.com/JSNN15/conveyor_crack_detection_2026)
(commit `73cc79f`, files `model.onnx.part0` and `model.onnx.part1` joined in that order). It is licensed under the
**GNU Affero General Public License v3.0**; the full text is in `LICENSE-AGPL-3.0.txt` next to this file. Source,
sha256 and model details are in `model_source.json`.

The model was trained with Ultralytics YOLOv8, which is also AGPL-3.0. Anyone who distributes this model, or offers
it to users over a network, must do so under the AGPL-3.0 terms.

The BeltCrack dataset is by Huang, Ji, Ma and Ye (*Pattern Recognition*, 2026,
doi:[10.1016/j.patcog.2026.113598](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2026.113598); data:
doi:[10.57760/sciencedb.31181](https://doi.org/10.57760/sciencedb.31181)).

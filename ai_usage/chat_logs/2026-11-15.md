## The fix

Nothing here makes the model drift-robust yet. Two options:

- **Wider validation:** more validation scenarios that span the full noise, drift and shared-noise ranges, so the threshold is tuned on conditions like test. This is the cheaper fix, but it only moves the threshold up. It may raise false-alert precision at the cost of recall.
- **Drift-robust features:** features that separate a slow sensor wander from a growing fault, for example per-sensor baselines or a section-level correction. This is the real fix and takes longer.
